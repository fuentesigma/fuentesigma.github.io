  • Evolving the dark universe as a single wave

    2014 – 2026

    A solver for the Schrödinger–Poisson equations in an expanding universe. Pseudo-spectral integration on the root grid, finite differences on adaptively refined patches, GPU and MPI support. Started as a master’s thesis at CINVESTAV-IPN and rebuilt since.

  • A Stokes law for connections that permute

    2025 – 2026

    An unordered fibre of N states at each point of a surface, neighbours matched by optimal transport, and the permutation that comes back after a loop. It started as a labelling problem in an ensemble of neural networks and ended as a theorem about exceptional points in non-Hermitian bands.

  • (Machine) Learning in the trap field of a confined ion

    2024

    Supervised learning embedded in the exactly solvable motion of a trapped ion. The time-varying trap field replaces the parametrised circuit as the learning hypothesis, and regression and classification acquire closed-form predictors in the canonical observables.
